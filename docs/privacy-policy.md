# KnowledgeForge AI Privacy Policy

## Data we process

KnowledgeForge AI processes the email address and password hash needed for account
authentication, documents uploaded by the tenant, extracted document text and
embeddings, queries submitted to the API, citations, operational logs, and usage
estimates.

## Why we process it

We process this data to authenticate users, isolate tenant data, retrieve relevant
document passages, generate grounded answers, provide citations, enforce quotas, and
operate the service safely.

## Retention and deletion

Account deletion is a hard-delete operation. It removes the tenant, users, documents,
chunks, embeddings, request logs, failed-ingestion records, and configured raw storage
objects. Backups and provider-managed logs may retain data until their documented
retention period expires.

## Sharing

Uploaded content is sent only to configured processing providers required to perform
embedding or generation. We do not sell uploaded content or authentication data.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to `privacy@knowledgeforge.ai`.

This document is a product draft and must be reviewed for the operating jurisdiction
before public launch.
